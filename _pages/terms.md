## Privacy Policy

The privacy of my visitors is important. This page explains the external service used to provide the public visitor map.

### Global Visitor Map

The footer uses a free [Flag Counter](https://www.flagcounter.com/) image to display aggregate visits by country or region. Loading this image connects the visitor's browser to Flag Counter.

According to [Flag Counter's Privacy Policy](https://www.flagcounter.com/privacy.html), the free counter determines an approximate country or region from the visitor's IP address, does not set cookies, and uses an encrypted combination of the IP address and counter identifier for up to seven days to reduce duplicate counting. The public map displays aggregated geographic counts rather than precise visitor locations.

This site does not currently use Google Analytics or an active commenting service.
